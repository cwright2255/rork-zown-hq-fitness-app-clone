ZOWN HQ

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

This policy explains what ZOWN HQ collects, why we collect it, who it is shared with, and the choices you have. ZOWN HQ is an early (alpha) release, so features and this policy may change.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account: your name and email address. Your password is handled by Firebase Authentication; we never see it. If you sign in with Apple, Google or Meta instead, that service confirms who you are to Firebase and we receive your name and email address, plus your profile picture from Google or Meta. We never see your Apple, Google or Meta password.
  • Profile and body metrics: age, gender, height, weight, goals, and body measurements you enter or that a body scan estimates. Body-scan photos are analyzed on your device. We save the resulting measurements and estimates, not the photos.
  • Fitness activity: workouts, sets, runs, walks and hikes, steps, and recovery data (sleep, resting heart rate and heart-rate variability) if you connect Apple Health or a wearable. A daily record of these readings is saved to your account, so the trend charts on the Health page come back on a new phone.
  • Location: GPS points while you record a run, walk or hike (including in the background while a recording is active), and your approximate location when you look up weather or nearby trails. For the Local 5K virtual race, a rough map square (about 7 miles across) for where your run started, and the squares around where you are when you look at its board; never your exact position. Only with your permission.
  • Nutrition: food and water logs, meal plans, and recipes you save or import.
  • Calendar: the events you schedule (title, day and time, notes, how often it repeats, and when to remind you), and the workouts and meals the AI coach adds to it after you say yes. They are saved to your account so a new phone gets them back. Reminders are set on your phone.
  • Social content: your public name and avatar, posts, comments, photos or videos you post, messages, who you follow, and challenge, duel or virtual race activity (your finishing times).
  • Device and diagnostics: crash reports (with your account ID attached), device model, operating system and app version, usage events, and a notification token if you turn on notifications.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To run the app: your history, plans, goals, progress, streaks, XP and rewards.
  • To power social features such as the leaderboard, feed, groups, messages and duels.
  • To send reminders and alerts you have turned on.
  • To find and fix crashes and bugs.

We do not sell your personal information and we do not use it for advertising. Data from Apple Health is used only to provide fitness features inside ZOWN HQ.

3. AI Features

The AI coach, the daily Today brief, workout and challenge generation, nutrition suggestions, progress summaries, body-composition insights, and the photo food scanner send the information they need (for example your goals, age, measurements, the text you type, or the meal photo you take) through our servers to OpenAI, which generates the response. The coach also keeps a short list of notes about you from your chats, such as how many days a week you can train (see Settings, then AI Coach, then What your Coach remembers, where you can read it, remove a note, forget everything or pause it); the list is saved to your account, sent to the AI with your messages, and used in the chats and in the Today brief. The Today brief is written once a day from what the coach already knows about you: your recent sleep, heart rate, heart-rate variability and weight, your training, your food log and your upcoming calendar items. We do not store meal photos you send for scanning. AI output can be wrong and is not medical advice.

4. Services That Receive Your Data

  • Google Firebase: sign-in, database, file storage, cloud functions, crash reporting (Crashlytics) and analytics.
  • Apple, Google and Meta: only if you choose to sign in with them. They learn that you signed in to ZOWN HQ, and what they do with that is covered by their own privacy policies.
  • OpenAI: AI features described above.
  • Radar, Google Places, TrailAPI and the U.S. National Weather Service: your coordinates or search text, to show a place name, find trails and places, and show weather.
  • OpenStreetMap (Overpass): the map point of a trail you open, to find its marked paths. Not your own location.
  • CalorieAPI and Spoonacular: food searches, barcodes you scan, and recipe links you import.
  • Exercise and muscle-diagram providers: exercise search terms and muscle groups. No personal profile data.
  • Our 3D body-model service: your height, weight, gender, body-fat estimate and waist and hip measurements, to draw your body model.
  • Spotify and Apple Music: only if you connect them, to control playback.
  • Apple Health and ROOK: only if you connect them, to bring in health and wearable data you choose to share.

5. What Other Users Can See

Your public name and avatar, posts and comments are visible to other signed-in users. Your leaderboard entry (name, avatar, XP, level, streak and distance) is visible unless you turn off Show in Leaderboards in Settings. So are your virtual race results on the race boards: your name, avatar, finishing time and, for the Local 5K, the rough map square where you started. Turning off Show in Leaderboards takes them off the boards. Messages are visible to the people in the conversation. Your workouts, body metrics, nutrition logs, calendar and virtual journey progress are private to your account.

6. Keeping and Deleting Your Data

We keep your data while your account is active. You can delete your account at any time in Settings > Danger Zone. Deleting it permanently erases your email address, profile, workouts, nutrition, calendar and body-scan records, AI coach chats and the coach's notes about you, posts, comments, likes, messages, duels, challenge entries, virtual race times, uploaded photos and videos, and your leaderboard entry from our servers, and then removes your sign-in. Conversations and duels you shared with other people are removed for them too. This cannot be undone.

A few things sit outside our servers. Firebase keeps crash reports for a limited time. OpenAI may keep AI requests for a short period under its own policy. Anything you connected to Spotify, Apple Music, Apple Health or ROOK stays in those services, where you can remove it yourself. If you signed in with Apple, deleting your account also disconnects ZOWN HQ from your Apple ID (you confirm with Apple first), so it no longer appears in the apps using your Apple ID. If you signed in with Google or Meta, ZOWN HQ may stay on the list of apps connected to that account after you delete yours; you can remove it in that service's settings. To get a copy of your data before you delete it, email privacy@zownhq.com from the address on your account.

7. Security

Your data is stored with Google Firebase, which encrypts it in transit and at rest. Our database rules limit private records to your own account. No system is perfectly secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Your Choices

  • Turn location, camera, photos, Apple Health and notification access on or off in your device settings at any time.
  • Control who can find you and see your activity under Settings > Privacy, and hide yourself from leaderboards.
  • Disconnect Spotify, Apple Music or wearables in Settings.
  • When you sign in with Apple you can choose to hide your email address; Apple then gives us a private forwarding address instead.

9. Children's Privacy

ZOWN HQ is not for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from them, and we will remove any account we learn belongs to a child under 13.

10. Cookies and Analytics

The app does not use web cookies. It uses Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics to measure how the app performs and to find crashes.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy. When we make an important change we will tell you in the app, and the date at the top will change.

12. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, email privacy@zownhq.com. For general support, email support@zownhq.com.
